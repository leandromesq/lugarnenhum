## Visual identity
Full-viewport band photography, white transparent ASCII wordmark, illustrated symbol, Consolas navigation and body text, Roboto Mono ASCII overlay. Electric blue announcement strip across desktop pages. Preserve the source palette. ## Visual effects

Desktop home uses one shared photographic scene with `cover` scaling. ASCII coordinates are calibrated in the original 1920 × 1280 image: x=-42, y=26 (the Pencil 1920 × 1080 crop removed 100 px from the top), 24 px font, 14.52 px column advance and 25.92 px row advance. Apply the same centered cover projection to the image and characters at every desktop viewport; do not position the overlay independently with vh/vw.

Hover applies a mild radial expansion in a 55 CSS-pixel radius. Within 80% of the radius, characters alternate through expanded, approximately density-matched punctuation alphabets at independent 180–360ms intervals with 80ms crossfades. Hash each cell and beat independently for irregular symbol choices, never immediately repeating a symbol; keep the glitch localized rather than flashing the entire image. The outer region keeps a stable replacement; the fisheye does not pulse. Use a dry circular boundary with binary glyph activation, no radial opacity ramp, and immediate entry/exit instead of the former 11px feather and 100/220ms crossfades. Select by glyph center and replace each active glyph in full, even when its ink crosses the radius. Never circularly clip a character or paint its original underneath the replacement. Cache the inactive glyph layer, rebuilding only when the active set changes, and draw just the active full glyphs over it. Use the resting cache on exit; preserve the natural geometric fisheye falloff and the 80ms glitch crossfades inside. Smooth only pointer tracking. Cache the resting canvas. Animate only while transitions/lens movement settle, sleep with a timer between character beats, and stop all frames/timers outside the interaction or when the document is hidden. SVG preserves the base composition without JavaScript. Reduced motion retains static ASCII.

The owner supplied `mobius strip vantablack.glb`, replacing the original metallic model and intermediate black edition, for a small black 3D object above the home navigation only. Preserve the supplied materials and add a flat, fully opaque white outline using Three.js `OutlinePass`, matching the official logo's graphic ink. Use full-resolution edge detection with a 1.5 CSS-pixel stroke, replace the pass's Gaussian blur stage with a direct copy, and threshold the overlay to binary white/transparent; no feather, pulsing or glow. Keep the post-processing output transparent and use a low-DPR WebGL canvas. Load Three.js and the self-contained GLB lazily when visible. Treat it as a fidget, not an orbit viewer. Start with a slow idle spin; hover/focus must never pause it. Directly rotate the object with screen-aligned quaternion increments: right drags turn right and upward drags turn upward. On release, retain the gesture's axis, direction and speed with exponentially decaying momentum; decelerate naturally. After 3 seconds without drag/keyboard interaction, smoothly restore the original orientation over 900ms, then resume the standard 0.18 rad/s idle spin. New interaction interrupts the return, and a held pointer must never trigger it. Hover does not reset the timer. Escape is an explicit stop until another interaction; reduced motion disables automatic return/resume. Capture the pointer to keep dragging outside the canvas; holding still before release cancels a fling. Support touch, keyboard arrows and Escape to stop. Track pointer versus keyboard focus explicitly: pointer/touch interaction must never show a square canvas border, including first programmatic focus; show the focus ring only for keyboard navigation and controls. With reduced motion, allow direct manipulation only, without idle spin or inertia. No pause button, by owner request. Disable automatic motion with reduced motion, stop drawing offscreen/when hidden, and dispose pointer listeners, idle timers, model textures/geometries, environment and renderer on navigation. Offer a small nonblocking “Arraste para girar” hint above the model for five seconds after it loads, dismissing it immediately on pointer or keyboard manipulation. On desktop, place the model and navigation in one intrinsic-width column, centering the model above the menu with an 18px gap rather than aligning their right edges independently. Place it above the bottom menu on mobile without covering links; gracefully report unavailable rendering/downloads without blocking navigation.

Keep the approved mobile portrait without ASCII or an announcement banner.

Each member in the home photograph is a clickable hotspot. Store the rectangles as fractions of the photograph's own pixels and project them with the same centred cover transform as the ASCII glyphs, so the hotspots move with the photograph at every viewport and the desktop set swaps for the mobile set when the art-directed source changes. ## Provisional screens
Music: six 3D CDs from `public/assets/models/cd/cd.glb` share one normalised model and take their printed label from `public/assets/models/cd/<release.id>.webp`; the label mesh is rotated to face the camera. A visible equirectangular sky and matching HDR reflections follow the visitor's local hour when the page opens: morning 06–10, day 10–16, sunset 16–19 and night 19–06. Only the current period loads; the page stays black until WebGL is ready or if it fails. The visible sky is a CSS layer behind the transparent WebGL canvas: one band of the panorama above the hazy horizon, dimmed, covering only the stretch away from the sun so text keeps its contrast. It drifts sideways and back over five minutes each way to suggest a turning sky; the drift is a compositor transform that never redraws the discs, whose HDR lighting stays fixed by owner decision, and it rests with reduced motion. Keep comments in `globals.css` ASCII-only: a degree sign inside a comment made the CSS pipeline drop the rules that followed it. Rebuild the four ambientCG assets with `node scripts/prepare-sky-assets.mjs`; source and license details live in `public/assets/sky/SOURCES.md`. The selected disc stays centred with a clear gap before a queue of overlapping discs on its left; the queued discs wait on the same level, each 99% the size of the one in front with spacing shrinking by the same ratio, and all turned about 29° towards the selection so their parallel faces read as standing one behind another on the same horizon (owner reference); the selected disc faces the viewer. A critically damped cursor carries the discs between those slots; all inactive discs wait in the left stack, with the next release at its front. The next CD slides into the centre while the outgoing one recedes behind the entire stack before joining its far end. No resting disc sits behind the selected disc or shows through its hole. Spin accelerates gradually and coasts down before returning upright. A primary click on the centre of the stage plays a 15-second preview (`public/assets/audio/<release.id>.mp3`), a click on either side steps, and swipes, horizontal wheel and arrow keys also navigate. Each disc keeps its own spin speed: the outgoing one coasts down while the next starts from rest when its preview becomes audible. Play fades in over 350 ms, pause fades out over 200 ms, and a playing track change fades out over 250 ms then leaves 650 ms of silence before the next preview. The volume slider starts at 25%; browsers that ignore media volume use a Web Audio gain stage created on the first play gesture. Paused navigation never starts sound, and a pause or newer selection cancels pending playback. During playback the announcement reads "TOCANDO AGORA - LUGAR NENHUM - <TITLE>.WAV". The circular label image replaces the 3D disc while loading or when WebGL fails. There is no autoplay; rendering is on demand and reduced motion disables drift, spin and tilt. Keep the native expandable tracklist for direct selection. Shop: simple coming-soon screen. No fake playback or checkout.
