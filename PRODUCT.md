# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Fans discovering Lugar Nenhum and returning listeners looking for music, merchandise, and information about the band.

## Product Purpose
Implement the band's approved Pencil document as a navigable website. The home and about compositions are the source of truth, including separate mobile imagery. Music and shop are provisional references, confirmed by the owner; use a simple CD carousel for music. Keep Lorem ipsum on the about page until the owner supplies the biography.

## Brand Personality
Photographic, pixelated, playful. The band's actual photos, ASCII wordmark, illustrated symbol, and blue announcement strip establish the voice.

## Anti-references
Do not retain the previous generic landing page, neon cards, invented brand imagery, or unrelated artists from the reference carousel.

## Design Principles
- Preserve the approved visual identity rather than redesign it.
- Give imagery the full viewport and keep navigation unobtrusive.
- Separate provisional content from real availability.
- Keep content replaceable without changing page architecture.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful image alternatives, readable mobile text, and reduced-motion support. Improve interaction usability without changing the visual direction.
